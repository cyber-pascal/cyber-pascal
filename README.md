### About Me

I'm a Junior Cybersecurity Analyst based in Hamburg, Germany, focused on penetration testing, threat detection, and vulnerability assessment. My work centers on identifying and validating security weaknesses across networks and systems, and translating findings into clear, actionable risk assessments.

Before moving into IT security, I spent over six years as an Aircraft Mechanic at Lufthansa Technik under strict EASA Part-145 standards, later working as a Manufacturing Engineer and Technical Trainer authoring SOPs and technical training programs. That background in regulated, audit-driven environments shaped the analytical mindset and attention to detail I now bring to security work.

I hold the **Google IT Support Professional Certificate** and the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate**, and I'm currently deepening my hands-on skills through the Masterschool Cybersecurity Training Program while working toward my **CompTIA Security+** certification.
